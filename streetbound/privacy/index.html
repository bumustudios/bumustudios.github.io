Streetbound Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Streetbound ("the game") is made by BumuStudios ("we", "us"). This page says what the game does with information. It is written to be read, not to hide things.

The short version

  • The game has no account of its own. The only sign-in is Google Play Games, which is Google's and optional. Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • We do not run a server for the game and we do not receive your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob and its mediation partners) and uses Firebase for crash reports and usage statistics. Those services collect some technical information, described below.
  • The game is for people 13 years of age or older. It is not directed at children.
  • You can switch crash reports and usage statistics off in the game (main menu, Privacy) and you can change your advertising choices at any time.

What stays on your device

Your save game, settings (volume, language, button layout, difficulty) and the record that you bought Remove Ads are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What third-party services collect

The game contains software from other companies. They collect information themselves, under their own privacy policies. We list each one here.

Advertising: Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize

The game shows banner ads in the main menu, short full-screen ads at calm moments (never during a fight or a boss), and optional ads that you choose to watch to wake up without a money penalty. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob mediation may let Unity Ads or Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) serve an ad instead.

These services may collect and use: your device's advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and software information (model, operating system version, language), and information about ad interactions and app performance. They use it to show ads, limit how often you see one, measure results, prevent fraud and keep the service running.

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff: https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form the first time it starts, before any ad is requested. You can reopen your choices later from the game (main menu, Privacy, Ad choices). You can also limit ad personalisation in your device's Google settings or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Ad content. We set the maximum ad content rating to Teen (T), because the game is for people 13 and older.

Crash reports and usage statistics: Firebase

The game uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase. Crashlytics records a report when the game crashes (the crash trace, device model, operating system version, free memory, and an installation identifier). Analytics records a few game events (for example "level reached", "boss defeated", "district entered", "ad shown", "purchase") with a pseudonymous app instance identifier. We use them to find and fix crashes and to see which parts of the game people reach. We do not combine them with your identity, because we do not know it.

Crash reports are on by default. You can turn off crash reports and usage statistics in the game (main menu, Privacy). In regions where consent is required, usage statistics wait for your consent. Firebase keeps Analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

  • Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Achievements and leaderboards: Google Play Games

Since version 0.3.4 Streetbound works with Google Play Games, a Google service. If Play Games is on your phone and you use it, the game signs you in to it automatically with the Play Games profile Google already holds for you. You can also sign in from the Play Games page in the game's settings, or never. Nothing in the game depends on it.

When you are signed in, the game sends Google Play Games the achievements you unlock and three scores: your total experience, the total K you have earned, and your fastest Copperhill clear time. Google keeps them with your Play Games profile and shows the leaderboards, with your Play Games name and picture, to other players according to your profile's visibility settings. Nothing else about your play is sent, and we receive no personal data from it, only what Google shows publicly on the leaderboards and aggregated statistics.

Your Play Games profile, its visibility and its data are managed in the Google Play Games app and your Google Account, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases and updates: Google Play

The one purchase, Remove Ads, is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. The game asks Google Play whether you own Remove Ads, so it can restore it after a reinstall. The game also uses Google Play's in-app review card and in-app update prompts, which talk only to the Play Store app on your device.

  • Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Why we use information, and the legal bases

  • Showing and measuring ads, after your consent where required (consent).
  • Keeping the game stable with crash reports (our legitimate interest in a working game, or your consent where the law requires it).
  • Understanding which parts of the game are played, with usage statistics (your consent, or our legitimate interest where the law allows it).
  • Selling and restoring Remove Ads (performing the purchase contract with you).

Retention

We hold no personal data of our own. The services above keep what they collect for the periods in their own policies: Firebase Analytics up to 14 months, Crashlytics 90 days, advertising partners as stated in their policies.

Your rights and choices

Because we receive no data that identifies you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. The services above may hold identifiers tied to your device. You can:

  • turn off crash reports and usage statistics in the game, and reopen your ad consent choices;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads);
  • ask the services directly, through the links above, to access or delete data tied to your advertising ID or device;
  • uninstall the game, which stops all collection.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Turkey (KVKK) or another region with data protection rights, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Streetbound is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The game does not direct ads to children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, tell us and we will help you use the controls above.

International transfers

Google, Unity and Liftoff operate worldwide, so information may be processed in countries other than yours, under the safeguards those companies describe in their policies.

Changes

If the game's use of information changes, we will update this page and its date. A change that needs your consent will ask for it in the game.
