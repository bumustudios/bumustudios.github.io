Tower defense on Android where strategy, not luck, wins

Enemies march down a winding road toward your fort. Build towers beside it, upgrade them, send in a hero and hold the line through ten waves, with a dragon at the end of every map. There are no hidden dice: the same defense always plays out the same way.

Free on Google Play for Android 7.0 or newer. The game shows ads; a one-time purchase removes them all.

What you get

  • Four towers, three levelsVolt, Pierce, Ravager and Glacier, each upgraded twice. Sell any tower for 70% of what you spent on it.
  • Four heroesFire, Ice, Nature and Rock: free, cannot be killed, and each has one big skill you can use twice a battle.
  • Six maps, ten waves eachEvery campaign map has its own road and biome, a dragon at the end and up to three stars.
  • Daily siegeA new battle every day, the same for everyone. Win day after day to build a streak.
  • Strategy, not luckNo random critical hits and no hidden dice: every battle is fully deterministic.
  • Plays offlineEvery map and the daily siege work without an internet connection.
  • No energy, no loot boxesPlay as many battles as you like. No account and no sign-in either.
  • No subscriptionFree with ads. One optional, one-time purchase removes every ad for good.

How to play Fortify

  1. Pick a map, or the Daily siege, and choose your hero. Enemies will walk the road toward your keep.
  2. Pick a tower from the bar at the bottom, then tap or drag it onto a free tile beside the road.
  3. Place your hero with its card; tap it later to move it. When you are ready, tap Start wave.
  4. Every kill pays gold. Tap a tower to upgrade or sell it, use the hero's skill during a wave (twice a battle, 40 seconds apart) and tap Next wave early for bonus gold.
  5. Survive all 10 waves to win. Your keep has 6 lives: an enemy that gets through costs 1, a golem 2 and the dragon 4. Keep 4 or more for three stars.

Towers, heroes and enemies

  • Towers. Volt fires fast at short range, but armor blunts it. Pierce sends heavy long-range bolts straight through armor. Ravager blasts a stretch of road. Glacier slows everything it hits.
  • Heroes. Fire burns, Ice slows, Nature strikes fast and Rock hits hard. Their skills are Firestorm, Deep Freeze, Bramble Surge and Earthshatter.
  • Enemies. Slimes, skeletons, bats, goblins, spiders, armored orcs, golems and the dragon. From wave 3 some come red and Enhanced, a third tougher; from wave 6 gold Elites join them, twice as tough. Both pay more gold.
  • Maps. The Bend, The Spiral, The Gauntlet, Frost Pass, Dune Road and Ember Hollow: meadow, forest, rocks, snow, desert and embers. Each win unlocks the next map.
  • Pace. Three speeds (1x, 1.5x, 2x) and pause. Leave a battle and tap Continue on the home screen to pick it up again.

Questions people ask

How do you play Fortify?

Build towers beside the road that enemies walk toward your keep, upgrade them with the gold you earn and place a hero to fight beside them. Survive all ten waves of a map to win. The full rules are under How to play on the home screen.

Is Fortify free?

Yes. The game is free and paid for by ads: a banner on the home screen and on the victory and defeat panels, a full-screen ad when you leave a finished battle (never two within 45 seconds), and an ad when you come back to the game from another app. No ad is ever shown on the board while you play.

How do I remove the ads?

Settings has a Remove ads option: one optional, one-time purchase that removes every ad for good. There is no subscription. Google Play keeps the purchase, so it comes back if you reinstall with the same Google account.

Can I play Fortify offline?

Yes. Every map and the daily siege can be played without an internet connection. The internet is used only for ads, for Google Play purchases and updates, and for the usage statistics and crash reports sent to Google Firebase.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your phone: unlocked maps, stars, best scores and your daily streak. There is no cloud save: progress is not included in Android backups, so it does not move to a new phone and is deleted if you uninstall the game.

Do I need an account? What data does it collect?

No account and no sign-in. Your battles and progress never leave the phone. Google's ads and the Firebase usage statistics and crash reports collect some device data, such as the advertising ID and an approximate location from the IP address; the privacy policy lists all of it.

Is it pay to win? Are there energy limits or loot boxes?

No. There is no energy system, no loot boxes and nothing to buy except the ad removal. Battles have no randomness, so the same defense always gives the same result: a better plan is the only way to win.

How do I get three stars on a map?

Win with 4 or more of your keep's 6 lives left. A win with 3 lives gives two stars, and any other win gives one. Every win unlocks the next map, so you can come back for the stars later.

What is the daily siege?

A new battle every day: one of the six maps, with enemies made tougher by an amount that changes from day to day. Everyone gets the same siege on the same date, worked out on your phone from the date itself. Win day after day to build your streak.

Any tips for a hard wave?

Pierce goes straight through armor, so it handles orcs, golems and the dragon. Glacier slows a crowd so the other towers can hit it, and Ravager hits a stretch of road at once. Move your hero to where the road is under pressure, save its skill for a big wave, and call the next wave early only when your defense is ahead.

Which languages is the game in?

The game is in English and Turkish. It follows your phone's language, and Settings lets you switch.

Which devices does it run on? Is there an iPhone version?

Android 7.0 or newer, on phones and tablets, always in portrait. Fortify is an Android game only; there is no iPhone or web version.
