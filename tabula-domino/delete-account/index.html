Delete your account — Tabula Domino

Tabula Domino: Online Oyna, by BumuStudios — Android, package com.bumustudios.domino

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

You can delete your Tabula Domino account and its data in two ways: inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app. Either way, your account is frozen first and deleted after 14 days; until then you can change your mind.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Tabula Domino and tap Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. The Delete your account screen shows what happens and what stays. If you like, first download a copy of your data from there.
  4. Confirm with Press and hold to delete my account, or tap Cannot press and hold? Confirm by typing, type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Your account is frozen at once: you cannot play or change your name, and you no longer appear in friend lists. The app shows the date and time of the deletion. To cancel, open the app within the 14 days and tap Cancel the deletion; everything comes back as it was. When the 14 days are over, the deletion is carried out automatically.

Ask for deletion without the app

If you have uninstalled the game or lost your phone, write to bumustudios@gmail.com from any email address, with the subject "Delete my Tabula Domino account". Include:

  • your player tag (for example #K7M2Q9XA), shown in the app under Settings → Account → Account and profile, next to "Your player tag";
  • your display name, and whether you linked Google, Apple, Facebook or X.

Your account has no email address attached, so before deleting anything we check that the account is yours. For example, we may ask you to sign in with the Google, Apple, Facebook or X account you linked, or to send a short code we give you through the in-app form under Settings → Account → Support and appeals. We never ask for a password. Once ownership is confirmed, we start the same deletion as in the app, tell you the date it takes effect, and you can still cancel by replying within the 14 days. We answer every request within 30 days at the latest.

A guest account that was never linked can only be opened from the phone it was created on. If you can no longer reach it, nobody else can either, and a guest account that is not used for 24 months is deleted automatically in the same way.

What is deleted

  • Your display name, including the copies in match records
  • Your player tag, avatar choice and profile photo
  • Your linked Google, Apple, Facebook or X sign-in, and all your sessions
  • Your name history
  • Your friends, friend requests, blocks and invites
  • Your quick-message records and matchmaking records
  • Your support requests and our replies
  • Your analytics consent answer

What is kept, and for how long

Some records are also part of other players' history or are needed for fair play and legal claims. They are kept without your name: your name and linked accounts are removed and only a random number remains.
