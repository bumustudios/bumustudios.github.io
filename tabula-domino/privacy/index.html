Privacy Policy — Tabula Domino

Online domino game for Android, package com.bumustudios.domino

Effective date: 17 October 2026. Last updated: 17 October 2026. Policy version 5.

No email address, no phone number, no real name. You play with a guest account and a display name you choose. Signing in with Google, Apple, Facebook or X is optional and only lets you get your account back on another device.

Tabula Domino is an online game, so your matches are played on our own servers, run by Hetzner in Germany (EU). This page lists everything those servers keep, why, and for how long. We do not sell your data.

Ads and Google services. The game shows ads from Google AdMob in the menus and, at most every third finished match, between matches; never during a match and never when the app opens. Ads are personalised only if you agree in Google's consent form. Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics) and usage measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase) are sent only if you allow them. Players aged 13 to 17 get none of the optional parts.

Akçe. The game's currency is only earned (by playing, from the daily gift and from rewarded ads you choose to watch). It cannot be bought, cashed out or sent to another player. Our servers keep a record of every change to your balance.

From version 1.0.3: an optional Ad-free Pack you can buy once through Google Play (Google takes the payment; we never see your card), notifications only if you allow them, and an automatic check of new profile photos that runs on our own servers.

What the game is

Tabula Domino is an online domino game. You are matched with other players by rating, or you open a private table with a code for friends. Matches, ratings and leaderboards are kept on our servers so that they are fair and so that a dropped connection does not lose your game. When no suitable opponent is found, you can play against computer opponents.

This version of the game

This policy describes the version of the game in Google Play's closed test. It contains:

  • Ads (Google AdMob). A small banner in the menus (home screen, profile, settings) and an ad between matches, at most once every third finished match and at least 3 minutes apart. No ads during a match or when the app opens. See Ads.

Profile photo check

Every new profile photo is checked automatically by an image model that runs on our own servers. It looks only for nudity and sexual content; it does not recognise faces, and the photo is not sent to anyone for the check. Then:

  • a photo that is clearly not allowed is refused and not stored; your previous photo stays;
  • if the result is not clear, the photo is shown only to you until someone on our team has looked at it and approved or refused it;
  • otherwise it is shown to other players.

We keep the model's score and result (approved, held for review, refused), the model's name, and our team's decision and its time. A stricter threshold applies if you are 13 to 17 or have not told us your age. Violence and other problems are handled through player reports and our team. If an image is suspected to be child sexual abuse material, we report it to the competent authorities as the law requires and keep it as evidence, shown to nobody, for as long as the law requires (at least 1 year), even if the account is deleted.

Notifications — only if you allow them

From version 1.0.3 the game can send notifications while you are away from it: when an opponent is found, your table is ready, it is your turn, your table wants a rematch, or a friend invites you to their table; players aged 18 or over may also get announcements about the game. On Android 13 and later the game asks first, and you can say no; on older Android versions notifications are on unless you turn them off in Android's settings. A notification carries nobody's name: an invitation says "A friend is inviting you to their table", and you see who when you open the game. Turn, invitation and announcement notifications do not come during the quiet hours you can set, and you can switch each kind off under Settings → Notifications.

When notifications are allowed, we keep a device record: the notification address (push token) that Google Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your phone, a random number for the installation, platform, app version, the phone's language and time zone. We also keep which kinds of notification you want, your quiet hours, and which notification was sent when. The notification address is created only after you allow notifications.

Purchases

From version 1.0.3 you can buy the Ad-free Pack once, under Settings → Purchases. Google Play takes the payment under its own terms; we never receive your card or bank details. Our server checks the purchase with Google Play and keeps a purchase record: a fingerprint (hash) of Google Play's purchase token (not the token itself), the product, the time, any refund, and that the pack is yours. The game gives Google Play a random account code with the purchase so that it can be restored on a new phone; it is not your player number and tells Google nothing else about you. Akçe can never be bought. Players aged 13 to 17 see an extra explanation before buying.

In-app analytics — only with your consent

The game can send usage events, such as "progress screen opened", with the app version, platform and a session number that changes on every launch, only if you allow it. The question appears in the app, and you can change your answer at any time under Settings → Privacy → Help us improve the game. These events go to our own servers. Your name, IP address, advertising ID, anything you type and the tiles in your hand never enter an analytics event. We keep your answer, the text version it was given for and its time, to respect and prove it.

On Android, with the same permission, Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC, acting on our behalf as data processor) also measures how the app is used: automatic events such as the app opening, session and engagement time, device model, operating system and app version, a random number for the installation, and a coarse location (country, city) derived from the IP address. No advertising ID is collected and none of this is used for ads. If you do not allow it, or withdraw it, nothing is sent to Firebase. Nothing is sent for players aged 13 to 17.

Separately, our servers count things that already happen on them, such as "match found" and "match finished", to measure how the service runs (number of matches, waiting times). If you decline analytics, or delete your account, these records are kept without your player number.

Crash reports — only with your permission

If you turn on Settings → Privacy → Crash reports (it starts off), a technical report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC, data processor) when the app crashes, so we can find the problem: the error trace, device model, operating system and app version, and a random number for the installation. The report does not include your name, your player number, anything you wrote or the tiles in your hand. Turning it off deletes reports that have not been sent yet. For now crash reports are sent on Android only, and never for players aged 13 to 17.

Ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited): a small banner in the menus (home screen, profile, settings), an ad between matches (at most once every third finished match, at least 3 minutes apart) and, where the app offers one, a rewarded ad that you watch only if you choose to. No ads are shown during a match or when the app opens.

AdMob processes your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. Your advertising ID is used for personalised ads only if you agree in Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform), which the game shows after your first finished match where the law requires a choice (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states). You can open it again at any time under Settings → Privacy → Ad choices and give or withdraw your consent. If you do not agree, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. In countries where no choice is asked (Türkiye included), ads are for now always non-personalised. Players aged 13 to 17 only get non-personalised ads, and the ad network is told that the player is a minor. Your advertising ID never reaches our servers or our analytics.

Rewarded ads. You may choose to watch one to refill your Akçe when it no longer covers the smallest table, or, after a lost match, to get half of your entry back. Before the ad, our server creates a random one-time reward number, and the app gives it to AdMob with the ad. When the ad has been watched, Google's server sends our server a message signed by Google with that number, and only then is the Akçe added. The reward number is not your player number and tells Google nothing about you.

Google's own use of the data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Technical records

  • IP address. Our application servers use it only in memory: to answer the request and, for at most an hour, as the key of a rate-limit counter that protects the service from abuse. It is never written to our database or linked to your account.
  • Edge logs. The network layer in front of our servers keeps access logs with the IP address and the app's identification string (user-agent) for 7 days, for security and to investigate faults, and then deletes them.
  • Monitoring logs. Our servers' error and monitoring logs may contain a player number, never names or session keys, and are kept for 14 days.
  • App version. Sent with every request so that the server can tell an outdated app to update.
  • Backups. Our database backups are kept for at most 16 days.

What stays on your phone

Your session key (in Android's secure storage), your sound, vibration and theme settings, the tips you have already seen, the target score you last chose, your answer to the age screen, and the choices you made in Google's ad consent form (kept on the phone by Google's consent library). The game reads your phone's language to show the interface in it. It asks for the camera or your photos only if you choose to set a profile photo, asks to show notifications (from version 1.0.3, on Android 13 and later), and requests no location, contacts or microphone permission. From version 1.0.2 none of this is copied to Android's cloud backup or to a new phone: the game switches Android's backup off, so uninstalling the game removes it all.

What we do not collect

Email address, phone number, real name, postal address, precise (GPS) location, your phone's contacts, your photos and files (other than a profile photo you choose), voice recordings, payment details, browsing history. We do not collect your advertising ID on our servers. We do not process special categories of personal data, such as health data.

What other players see

  • At the table: your display name, profile photo or avatar, country flag, your moves (never your hand), the quick messages you send, and the result.
  • Everyone: your display name, profile photo or avatar, country and rank on leaderboards and in friends lists. A profile photo is visible to other players once the automatic check has passed it (see Profile photo check); you can remove it at any time in your profile. Other players can report a photo they find inappropriate, and our team can hide or delete it.
  • Your friends: whether you are online or in a match, unless you turn this off.
  • Players you met recently, and anyone who knows your player tag: can send you a friend request.

Bots

When no suitable opponent is found, or in practice, you may play against computer opponents (bots). Bots are always labelled BOT next to their name and on the end-of-match screen; there are no hidden bots. Bot profiles are invented and do not belong to real people.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account is frozen at once and deleted after 14 days; during those 14 days you can open the app and tap Cancel the deletion, and everything comes back as it was. You can also ask for deletion by email without the app. Step-by-step instructions, and the full list of what is deleted and what is kept, are on the account deletion page.

  • Deleted: your display name (also the copies in match records), player tag, avatar choice, profile photo, linked sign-ins, sessions, name history, friends, requests, blocks, invites, quick-message records, matchmaking records, support requests, your analytics answer, your device records and notification settings. Your Akçe balance is closed and cannot be restored.
  • Kept without your name, under a random number only: match records and move logs (they are also the other players' records), statistics and rating totals, your Akçe record, purchase records, and records of team decisions, for the periods above. An image kept as evidence for the authorities (see Profile photo check) is kept for the period the law requires.
  • Player number removed as well: analytics events and server-side counts.
  • A report made about you stays until its period ends, with your player number replaced by a new number that cannot be traced back.
  • Deletion reaches our backups when they expire, within 16 days.
  • Crash reports and Firebase usage events are not linked to your player number; they expire at Google after 90 days and 14 months. Ad data is held by Google under its own policy.

Children and protected mode

The game is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 to play. Players aged 13 to 17 play in a protected mode: only non-personalised ads, with the ad network told that the player is a minor; no ad, analytics or crash-report consent prompt, so no in-app analytics, Firebase data or crash reports are sent; no announcement notifications; a stricter profile photo check; and an extra explanation before a purchase. We use the age information you give in the app only to apply these rules. If we learn that a player under 13 has an account, we delete it. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, write to the address below.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (TLS).
  • There are no passwords. Your session key is kept in your phone's secure storage; our server keeps only its hash, and session keys are never written to logs.
  • Your display name and linked sign-ins are kept in one part of the database; the other parts know only your player number.
  • Your hand and other hidden information stay on the server; your phone receives only what you are allowed to see.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects you, we will notify the authorities and you as the law requires.

Automated decisions

  • Matchmaking picks your opponent automatically, based on rating.
  • If you cancel many searches in a short time, or do not join matches you were placed in, matchmaking may make you wait a short time before searching again. This is not a sanction; you can still play private tables, and our team can lift it.
  • Profile photo check: a new photo may be refused automatically, or shown only to you until someone on our team has looked at it. You can challenge this in the app under Settings → Account → Support and appeals, and a person reviews it.
  • Reports never trigger an automatic sanction. Every suspension or ban is decided by a person on our team, and you can appeal it in the app.
