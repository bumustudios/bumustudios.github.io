Privacy Policy — Knight Fist

Android game, package com.bumustudios.knightfist

Effective date: 31 October 2026. Last updated: 31 October 2026.

No account, and the game plays offline. There is no account and no server belonging to this game; the only sign-in is Google Play Games, which is Google's and optional. The game does not read your files, your photos or your contacts, and it asks for no storage, camera, microphone or location permission. Every stage, fight and story scene runs on your phone.

What does leave your phone is described in full below: Google's ads, Google Play Games, and the usage statistics and crash reports that the developer collects through Google Firebase. There are no purchases in the game.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android game Knight Fist, distributed through Google Play under the package name com.bumustudios.knightfist. It is written by the game's independent developer. Questions can be sent to the address in Contact.

What the game is

Knight Fist is a medieval side-scrolling beat 'em up. You choose one of three heroes and fight through ten stages, from a village street to a castle's throne hall, against outlaws, soldiers and ten bosses, with a short story told between them. No account, no subscription, no energy system.

The game runs on your device

  • Your play is not uploaded. The game has no backend of its own. Your progress, your scores and an unfinished stage are kept on your phone. The only things about your play that leave it are the achievements and two leaderboard scores sent to Google Play Games if you use it, and the stage events counted by the usage statistics described below.
  • The game reads no files. It requests no storage, camera, microphone, contacts or location permission, on any Android version.

What the game stores locally

The game keeps one small save file in its own private storage. None of it is personal information:

  • your music, sound, screen-shake and vibration settings, the size of the touch controls, the difficulty and the language;
  • which stages are open, your best score, rank and time on each, and totals such as foes defeated and time played;
  • the hero you chose last and, when you leave a stage after a checkpoint, where to continue;
  • the Google Play Games achievements you have earned, kept until they have been sent, and your longest chain.

This save file, and nothing else the game or its components store, is included in Android's backup and in device-to-device transfer, so your progress can follow you to a new phone. Google's advertising and consent components keep their own settings on the device, under Google's control, and the Google Firebase components described below keep random identifiers in the game's private storage (a Firebase installation ID, the Analytics app-instance ID and a Crashlytics installation ID); these travel with the usage statistics and crash reports but are never backed up. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes everything it has stored.

The game is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, depending on your region). AdMob is embedded in the game as a software development kit, and it does collect data, independently of the developer. Achievements and leaderboards — Google Play Games

Knight Fist works with Google Play Games, a Google service. If Play Games is on your phone, the game signs you in to it automatically with the Play Games profile Google already holds for you; nothing in the game depends on it.

When you are signed in, the game sends Google Play Games the achievements you unlock and two scores: your best score on a stage and your longest chain of hits. Google keeps them with your Play Games profile and shows the leaderboards, with your Play Games name and picture, to other players according to your profile's visibility settings. Nothing else about your play is sent, and the developer receives no personal data from it. Your Play Games profile and its data are managed in the Google Play Games app and your Google Account, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

There are none. The game sells nothing and asks for no payment details.

Your advertising choices

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

Before any ad is requested, the game shows a consent form built with Google's User Messaging Platform, where you choose what you allow. Your choice is stored on your device by Google's component and respected on later launches. You can change it at any time from Settings → Privacy options. Withdrawing consent does not stop the game; you keep playing, and ads may still be shown in a non-personalised form.

Everywhere else

The privacy options entry appears only where a consent choice is legally required. You can still reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by manufacturer and Android version).

Permissions the game requests

  • INTERNET — to fetch ads, to talk to Google Play Games and to send the usage statistics and crash reports described above. Nothing else is sent over the network.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — lets the ad components check for a connection before loading.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — the advertising identifier, used by AdMob and by Google Analytics for Firebase as described above.
  • VIBRATE — short pulses on heavy blows, which you can turn off in Settings.

The Google components built into the game add a few more that Android grants at installation without asking you, such as the install-referrer and Android's advertising-services permissions; the game requests no storage, camera, contacts, microphone, location or account permission.

Children

Knight Fist is a fighting game for a general, teenage and adult audience. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), it is not listed in a children's category, and it is not designed for or marketed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ad, analytics or crash-reporting services in this game, contact the address below and contact Google, whose services would hold it.

Data retention

The developer runs no server and keeps no user data of its own. The usage statistics and crash reports are held by Google on the developer's behalf, for the periods given above. Data on your device stays there until you clear the game's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google through AdMob and Google Play Games is kept according to Google's own schedules, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR (EU/UK) applies, the legal basis for personalised advertising is your consent, collected through the form described above and withdrawable at any time. Non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention and the security of the ad services rely on legitimate interests. The usage statistics and crash reports rely on the developer's legitimate interest in keeping the game working and balanced; storing Analytics identifiers on your device and any advertising use of the data follow your choices in the consent form.

Under the GDPR, and under Turkey's KVKK (Law No. 6698) on equivalent terms, you have the right to ask whether your personal data is processed, to access it, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority — in Turkey, the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (KVKK). The usage statistics and crash reports are pseudonymous: they carry random identifiers, never your name or email address, so the developer cannot tell which records are yours. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data ends the link to the old identifiers. Requests concerning advertising or Play Games data must go to Google, using the tools in the Google Privacy Policy; for anything else, write to the address below.
