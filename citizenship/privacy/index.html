Privacy Policy — Citizenship Test

Android app, package com.bumustudios.citizenship (on Google Play as "US Citizenship Test")

Effective date: 15 November 2026. Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Controller: Burak Gökgöz (BumuStudios), Türkiye — bumustudios@gmail.com

Your study progress stays on your phone. There is no account to make, no sign-in and no server belonging to this app. Your answers, scores, state, interview date and the names you write down as your senators, representative and governor are kept only in the app's private storage, and they are not included in Android backup.

What does leave your device is described in full below: Google's advertising service and the ad networks it works with, Google's payment service, and the usage statistics and crash reports that the developer collects through Google Firebase — never your answers or your test details. If you buy Pro, the ads stop altogether; the usage statistics and crash reports do not depend on it.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android application Citizenship Test, distributed through Google Play under the package name com.bumustudios.citizenship. It is written by the app's independent developer. Questions can be sent to the address in Contact.

The controller of the personal data described in this policy — the party responsible for it under data protection law, including the GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK — is Burak Gökgöz, who publishes the app as BumuStudios, in Türkiye. Contact: bumustudios@gmail.com.

What the app does

Citizenship Test helps you study for the civics test of the US naturalization interview. It covers the 128 civics questions that U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) publishes, with the answers USCIS accepts, practice answer choices and explanations in English and Spanish, a button that reads each question aloud, full test simulations (including the 65/20 version), a list of the questions you missed, a readiness score and an English–Spanish glossary of civics terms.

Citizenship Test is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by USCIS, the U.S. Department of Homeland Security or any other government agency. Official information is at uscis.gov/citizenship.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its own private storage on the device. It is used only by the app on your phone and is not sent anywhere:

  • your study profile: the state you chose, whether the 65/20 test is on, your interview date, your daily goal, the language for explanations and whether English-only mode is on;
  • the answers you write down for the questions that depend on where you live: the names of your senators, your representative and your governor;
  • your progress: which questions you answered, how many times you got each one right or wrong, the questions answered per day (for the daily goal and the streak) and your test scores;
  • your theme, text size and app language choices;
  • whether Pro is active, and when an hour of Pro earned with a rewarded ad ends;
  • a counter of how many interstitial ads have been shown, used to keep ad frequency low.

The app does not take part in Android backup: its storage is excluded from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so a new phone starts with empty progress. Google's advertising and consent components also keep their own settings on the device, under Google's control, and the Google Firebase components described below keep three random identifiers in the app's private storage: a Firebase installation ID, the Analytics app-instance ID and a Crashlytics installation ID. Unlike the list above, these identifiers travel with the usage statistics and crash reports. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything the app has stored on the device, these identifiers included.

Purchases — Google Play Billing

The app offers one optional, one-time, non-consumable purchase, Citizenship Test Pro: no ads and unlimited full test simulations. There is no subscription and nothing recurring.

The transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. The developer never sees or receives your card number, bank details, billing address or full name. Google Play reports only aggregated sales figures, and Google Analytics for Firebase records that a purchase was made — product, name and price, with no payment details. Inside the app, the purchase is recorded as a single true/false flag on your device, restored from your Google Play account when you reinstall or move to a new device.

Updates — Google Play

The app uses Google Play's In-App Updates library. It asks Google Play whether a newer version of the app is available and, when there is one, lets Google Play offer it to you, download it and install it. It is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Listen — your phone's text-to-speech

The Listen button reads a question aloud with the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example Google's Speech Services), so you hear it the way the officer asks it. The app passes only the text of the question to that engine and ships no audio of its own. The engine is part of your phone, not of the app; depending on the engine and the voice, it may use a network connection to produce the speech, under its provider's privacy policy. Nothing about you or your progress is included.

Links to official websites

The app links to official pages where you can look up your senators, your representative and your governor (senate.gov, house.gov, usa.gov) and to the USCIS citizenship pages (uscis.gov). They open in your browser, and those websites' own privacy policies apply there.

When you write to us

Every question has a "Report a problem with this question" link, and More has a Contact entry. Both open your own email app with our address and, for a report, the question's number and text filled in; nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. When you do, we receive your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to answer you and to fix the question. We keep such emails as long as needed to handle them, and delete them on request.

Where a US state privacy law applies (for example in California), Google's consent tool can show a notice about the use of your information for targeted advertising and let you opt out of its sale or sharing. Where it applies, the More → Privacy choices entry appears and opens your choices again. Your rights are described under Privacy rights in US states below.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

The app is offered in the United States, but if you use it in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form built with Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are requested, where you choose what you allow, including for the ad partners listed above. You can change your decision at any time in More → Privacy choices. Withdrawing consent does not disable the app; ads may still be shown in a non-personalised form. Everywhere

You can control advertising at the operating-system level: open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer and Android version) to reset your advertising ID, or to delete it entirely, which stops apps from receiving a stable advertising identifier. Buying Pro stops ad requests from the app altogether; an hour of Pro earned with a rewarded ad stops them for that hour.

Permissions the app requests

  • INTERNET — required to fetch ads, to communicate with Google Play Billing and to send the usage statistics and crash reports described above.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — lets the ad components check whether a connection is available before trying to load.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — access to the advertising identifier, used by AdMob, its mediation partners and Google Analytics for Firebase as described above.

These are the permissions the app declares itself. The Google and ad-network components built into it add a few more that Android grants at installation without asking you, such as Google Play's billing permission and Android's advertising-services permissions; the app requests no storage, camera, contacts, microphone, precise location or account permissions.

The usage statistics and crash reports are held by Google on the developer's behalf: Analytics data linked to an installation's identifiers for no longer than 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days. Data held on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google through AdMob and Google Play is retained according to Google's own retention schedules, and data collected by a mediation partner according to that partner's policy, linked above.

Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR (EU/UK) applies, the legal basis for personalised advertising, including by the mediation partners, is your consent, collected through the form described above and withdrawable at any time. Non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention and the security of the ad services rely on legitimate interests. Processing your purchase is necessary to perform the contract you enter when you buy Pro. The usage statistics and crash reports rely on the developer's legitimate interest in keeping the app working and understanding, in aggregate, how it is used; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Analytics' storage of its identifier on your device and any advertising use of the data rely on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Answering your emails relies on our legitimate interest in replying to you.

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask whether your personal data is processed, to access it, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time, and you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country. The usage statistics and crash reports are pseudonymous: they carry random identifiers, never your name or email address, so the developer cannot tell which records are yours. Requests concerning advertising data must be directed to Google, or to the mediation partner concerned, as the party that holds it. For anything else, or for help finding the right route, write to the address below.
