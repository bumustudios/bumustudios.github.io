# bumustudios.github.io

The public site for **BumuStudios**, served by GitHub Pages at
<https://bumustudios.github.io/>.

It is plain, hand-written HTML. There is no build step, no framework and no
dependencies: every page carries its own `<style>` block. Edit the HTML, push to
`main`, and Pages republishes in about a minute.

## Read this before editing a policy page

These are not marketing pages. They are the legal documents that

- are **compiled into the shipped apps** — `SettingsScreen.kt` in each app opens
  its policy URL directly, so an app already installed on someone's phone points
  at whatever is live here, and
- are **declared to Google Play** as each app's privacy policy URL. Play re-checks
  that URL after publication and can suspend a listing over a dead link or over
  content that contradicts the Data Safety form.

Consequences of that:

- **Never move or rename a policy directory.** Old app versions cannot be
  updated to follow you.
- **Change both languages together.** A Turkish page that describes different
  data handling than the English one is a discrepancy, not a translation lag.
- **Update the "last updated" date** in the page when the substance changes, and
  keep it identical across the two languages.
- Keep the apps' pages structurally parallel. They are deliberately the same
  template, which is what makes a difference between them readable as meaningful.
- **Name every ad network the app can serve ads from.** The apps published from
  October 2026 (Mahjong Solitaire onwards) use AdMob mediation with AppLovin, Unity Ads,
  Liftoff, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral and Pangle; their policies list each one. If a
  network is added to or removed from an app's mediation, its policy changes with it.

## app-ads.txt

`app-ads.txt` sitenin **kökünde** durur: https://bumustudios.github.io/app-ads.txt

AdMob, bir uygulamanın mağaza kaydındaki **geliştirici web sitesi** alanından alan adını
alır ve dosyayı orada arar. Dosya alan adı başına birdir, uygulama başına değil — tek
satır bütün uygulamalarımızı kapsar. Bu yüzden iki koşul birlikte sağlanmalı:

1. Dosya kökte yayında kalmalı (alt klasöre taşınmamalı, silinmemeli).
2. Her uygulamanın Play Console mağaza kaydında **Web sitesi** alanı
   `https://bumustudios.github.io` olmalı.

Yeni bir reklam ağı eklenirse satırı buraya eklemek gerekir; AdMob tarafında
**Uygulamalar → app-ads.txt → Güncellemeleri kontrol et** ile tarama tetiklenir.

### Mediation ortakları

Mahjong Solitaire ve sonrasındaki uygulamalar AdMob mediation ile şu ağlardan da reklam
alır: **AppLovin, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Pangle**.
Her ağın hesabı açıldığında, o ağın panelinin verdiği app-ads.txt satır(lar)ı dosyadaki
ilgili yorumun altına eklenmeli — bizim yayıncı/hesap kimliklerimizle, başka yerden
kopyalanmadan. Satırı eksik olan ağ, Tier-1 alıcıların büyük kısmına "doğrulanmamış
envanter" görünür ve o ağdan gelen gelir belirgin düşer.

## Tabula Domino invitation page

`tabula-domino/join/?c=CODE` is the link the Tabula Domino app shares for a private table
(DominoOnline `docs/design/L1-davet-baglantisi.md`). It is **compiled into the app**: the app's
Android intent filter names `bumustudios.github.io` + `/tabula-domino/join`, and every shared
invitation carries this address. Never move or rename it.

- No cookies, no analytics, no third-party requests: the fonts (Alegreya and Alegreya Sans, SIL OFL
  1.1, subset to Latin; the licence texts sit beside them), the Tophane painting (public domain),
  the felt texture (CC0) and the app icon are served from `tabula-domino/join/assets/`.
- The code is checked against the server's alphabet and length; anything else shows the page
  without a code. Languages: tr, en, es, pt (from the browser; `?lang=` overrides).
- "Take your seat" (Android only) is `intent://join/CODE#Intent;scheme=domino;package=com.bumustudios.domino;S.browser_fallback_url=<Play>;end`.
  The Google Play button is the store page with `&referrer=join%3DCODE`: the app reads it once
  after install and opens the join screen with the code filled in.
- **App Links:** `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` names `com.bumustudios.domino` with two SHA-256
  fingerprints: the **Play app-signing key** (`65:94:EF:…:91:A6`, the one installs from Play carry) and
  the upload key (`14:6A:3E:…:48:DB`, for sideloaded release builds). Both come from Play Console >
  Test and release > App integrity > App signing. Public certificates, not secrets. Another studio app
  that wants App Links adds its own object to the same array; never remove an entry. `.nojekyll` is
  what lets Pages serve the dot-folder. Check: <https://digitalassetlinks.googleapis.com/v1/statements:list?source.web.site=https://bumustudios.github.io&relation=delegate_permission/common.handle_all_urls>.

## ImageToPdf landing page

`imagetopdf/` (English, x-default) and `imagetopdf/<lang>/` for tr, es, pt, hi, ar (RTL), de, fr are
the app's public page: what it does, how-to steps with store screenshots, an FAQ, and schema.org
`SoftwareApplication` + `FAQPage` JSON-LD. Added 2026-10-19 because almost all of the app's Play
visitors arrive from outside Play, a third of them from AI assistants.

- **Generated, not hand-edited.** The copy is in `Yayin Panosu/magaza-metinleri/imgtopdf/web/content.py`
  (YayinPanosu repo) and `build.py` there writes the eight `index.html` files:
  `python3 -I build.py <path-to-this-repo>`. Edit the copy there and rebuild, or the next build undoes
  a hand edit.
- **Only true claims.** Everything on the page is in the store listing or the app (1.2.4). When the app
  changes (a feature, the ads, the languages), update the page with it.
- **No scripts, no trackers, no external requests.** Images are in `imagetopdf/assets/`: WebP copies of
  the store screenshots, the icon, the feature graphics (og:image) and Google's official, unmodified
  "Get it on Google Play" badges per language (with the trademark line in the footer, as Google's badge
  guidelines require).
- **Play link** carries `utm_source=bumustudios_site&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=landing&utm_content=<lang>`
  (and `hl=` on the translated pages), so this page's traffic is visible in Play Console's UTM report.
  `llms.txt` and the JSON-LD use the plain Play URL on purpose, so AI-assistant referrals keep their own
  `utm_source`.
