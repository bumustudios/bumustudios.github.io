Privacy Policy — Arrow Rush

Android game, package com.bumustudios.arrowrush

Effective date: 27 August 2026. Last updated: 7 October 2026.

No account, and your progress stays on your phone. There is no sign-in and no server belonging to this game. The game does not read your files, your photos or your contacts — it never asks for a single storage permission. Everything, including the daily puzzle, is computed on your phone, offline.

What does leave your phone is described in full below: Google's advertising and payment services, and the usage statistics and crash reports that the developer collects through Google Firebase. If you buy the one-time ad removal, the interruptions stop; the only ads left are the optional ones you can choose to watch for a hint or for extra moves.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android game Arrow Rush, distributed through Google Play under the package name com.bumustudios.arrowrush. It is written by the game's independent developer. Questions can be sent to the address in Contact.

What the game is

Arrow Rush is an arrow-escape puzzle. Arrows sit on a grid, each pointing a direction; tap one whose path is clear and it flies off the board. Clear every arrow within the move limit to win. There are endless numbered levels and a daily challenge. No account, no subscription, no energy system.

The game runs entirely on your device

This is the most important section, so it is stated plainly:

  • Your moves and your progress are not uploaded. The game has no backend of its own. Your progress, your stars and your streaks are not transmitted anywhere and there is no online leaderboard. The usage statistics described below record when the game is opened, which screens are shown and for how long — never your moves, your stars or your progress.
  • The daily challenge needs no server. Everyone in the world gets the same board for the same date because it is derived mathematically from the date itself, on your device. No network request is involved.
  • The game reads no files. It requests no storage, camera, microphone, contacts or location permission — not on any Android version.
  • Levels are not generated from your play. Every board is a fixed, deterministic function of its level number or date, built before you ever see it. This is a fairness property rather than a privacy one, but it is part of the same promise: your moves are not analysed — not by the game, and not by the usage statistics described below, which never see them.

What the game stores locally

The game keeps a small amount of state in its own private storage on the device. None of it is personal information, and none of it is sent anywhere:

  • your theme, text size, language, sound and vibration choices;
  • your unlocked level, per-level stars and best scores, and your daily streak;
  • whether the one-time "remove ads" purchase is active;
  • a counter of how many interstitial ads have been shown, used to keep ad frequency low.

This local state is deliberately excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to your Google Drive. Google's advertising and consent components also keep their own settings on the device, under Google's control, and the Google Firebase components described below keep three random identifiers in the game's private storage: a Firebase installation ID, the Analytics app-instance ID and a Crashlytics installation ID. Unlike the list above, these identifiers travel with the usage statistics and crash reports. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes everything the game has stored, these identifiers included.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The game is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, depending on your region). AdMob is embedded in the game as a software development kit, and it does collect data, independently of the developer. When ads are served, AdMob and Google's advertising partners may collect and process:

DataUsed for
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier assigned by Android) Ad serving, frequency capping, ad measurement, fraud prevention
Approximate location — coarse, derived from your IP address, not GPS Serving ads relevant to your country or region, and legal compliance
Device information — model, operating system version, language, screen and network characteristics Rendering ads correctly, ad measurement, fraud prevention
App interaction events — that an ad was requested, shown, clicked or closed Ad measurement, billing advertisers, fraud prevention

The game does not request GPS or precise location permission, and it does not send your progress or your play history to AdMob. The developer receives no personal data from AdMob — only aggregated, anonymous reporting such as impression and revenue totals.

Because ads require an advertising identifier, the game declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission. Ad formats used are a banner on the menu and result screens, an occasional interstitial between levels, optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch (to reveal a hint, or to continue with extra moves), and an app-open ad. No ad is ever shown on the board while you are playing.

Usage statistics and crash reports — Google Firebase

To see how the game is used and to find and fix crashes, the developer has built two Google Firebase services into it: Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. Google provides them as the developer's data processor, and they are active only in the version of the game distributed through Google Play. The game adds nothing of its own to what they collect — no custom events, no user ID or user properties, no custom keys or log messages — so only their automatic collection applies:

DataUsed for
App-instance ID — a random identifier that Analytics creates for this installation Counting installations and sessions without knowing who you are
Advertising ID — the resettable identifier described above, available to Analytics because the game declares the AD_ID permission for AdMob Usage statistics; use for advertising only as far as your consent choices allow (below)
Approximate location — country or region, derived from your masked IP address, not GPS Statistics by country and region
Device and game information — device model, operating system version, language, game version Statistics by device, Android version, language and game version

Purchase events reported by Google Play — product, name and price of the one-time "remove ads" purchase Counting purchases; no card or payment details are included
Crash reports, sent only when the game crashes or hits a fatal error — the stack trace, the state of the game at that moment, device details (model, operating system version, orientation, free memory and disk space) and a random Crashlytics installation ID Finding and fixing crashes, and counting how many installations a crash affects

Analytics records that a screen was shown and for how long, not what you do on it, and a crash report shows where in the code the crash happened, not your content. Neither service receives your name, your email address or any account details.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the consent form described below under "Your advertising choices" also covers Analytics: your choices there decide whether Analytics may store its identifiers on your device and whether the data may be used for advertising. Use of the data for advertising ("ad user data") and for ad personalisation stays off there until you consent. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options; the entry appears where it is required. The game has no separate switch for the usage statistics or the crash reports; uninstalling it stops both.

Analytics keeps data linked to an installation's identifiers for no longer than 14 months; aggregated reports that contain no identifiers may be kept longer. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Google processes this data on the developer's behalf, as its data processor, under the Firebase and Google Analytics data processing terms. It may be processed on Google's servers outside your country, including in the United States, and it is encrypted in transit (TLS).

Purchases — Google Play Billing

The game offers one optional, one-time, non-consumable purchase that permanently removes the interrupting ads. There is no subscription and nothing recurring. The rewarded helpers remain available after the purchase — they only ever play when you ask.

The transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. The developer never sees or receives your card number, bank details, billing address or full name. Google Play reports only aggregated sales figures, and Google Analytics for Firebase records that a purchase was made — product, name and price, with no payment details (see above). Inside the game, the purchase is recorded as a single true/false flag on your device, restored from your Google Play account when you reinstall or move to a new device.

Google's handling of payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms.

Your advertising choices

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

Before personalised ads are requested, the game shows a consent form built with Google's User Messaging Platform, where you choose what you allow. Your choice is stored on your device by Google's component and is respected on later launches.

You can change your decision at any time: open the game, go to Settings → Privacy options, and the same form reappears. You may withdraw consent there. Withdrawing consent does not disable the game; you can keep playing, and ads may still be shown in a non-personalised form. The same choices also apply to Google Analytics for Firebase, as described above.

Everywhere else

The privacy options entry appears only where a consent choice is legally required, so you may not see it. You can still control advertising at the operating-system level: open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer and Android version) to reset your advertising ID, or to delete it entirely, which stops apps from receiving a stable advertising identifier.

Removing ads completely

Buying the one-time ad removal stops the banner, interstitial and app-open ads altogether. The only ads left are the rewarded helpers, which play only when you tap their buttons; the game loads one in advance so that it is ready.

Permissions the game requests

  • INTERNET — required to fetch ads, to communicate with Google Play Billing and to send the usage statistics and crash reports described above. Nothing else is sent over the network.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — lets the ad components check whether a connection is available before trying to load.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — access to the advertising identifier, used by AdMob and by Google Analytics for Firebase as described above.

These are the permissions the game declares itself. The Google components built into it add a few more that Android grants at installation without asking you, such as Google Play's billing permission and Android's advertising-services permissions; but the game requests no storage, camera, contacts, microphone, location or account permissions on any Android version.

The developer does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Data retention

The developer runs no server and keeps no user data of its own. The usage statistics and crash reports are held by Google on the developer's behalf: Analytics data linked to an installation's identifiers for no longer than 14 months (aggregated reports without identifiers may be kept longer), and crash reports for 90 days. Data held on your device stays there until you clear the game's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google through AdMob and Google Play is retained according to Google's own retention schedules, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR (EU/UK) applies, the legal basis for personalised advertising is your consent, collected through the form described above and withdrawable at any time. Non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention and the security of the ad services rely on legitimate interests. Processing your purchase is necessary to perform the contract you enter when you buy ad removal. The usage statistics and crash reports rely on the developer's legitimate interest in keeping the game working and understanding, in aggregate, how it is used; storing Analytics identifiers on your device and any advertising use of the data follow your choices in the consent form.

Under the GDPR, and under Turkey's KVKK (Law No. 6698) on equivalent terms, you have the right to ask whether your personal data is processed, to access it, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority — in Turkey, the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (KVKK).

The usage statistics and crash reports are pseudonymous: they carry random identifiers, never your name or email address, so the developer cannot tell which records are yours. Where the consent form is shown, you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options, and you can object to this processing, or ask about it, by writing to the address below. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data ends the link to the old identifiers, and data already sent then expires on the schedule above. Requests concerning advertising data must be directed to Google as the party that holds it, using the tools in the Google Privacy Policy. For anything else, or for help finding the right route, write to the address below.
